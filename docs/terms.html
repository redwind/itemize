Estimated values are estimates, not a valuation

Every value Inventa shows you — "estimated value today," depreciation, anything on the insurance report — is a straight-line estimate based on a conventional useful life for that category of item. It is arithmetic on a purchase price and a date, meant to help you sanity-check your cover. It is not a professional valuation, and it is not insurance, financial, tax, or legal advice. Your insurer may use a different depreciation schedule and reach a different figure.

The PDF report Inventa Pro generates is built to be useful to an insurer when you make a claim — but the estimated values in it remain estimates. If you need a figure you can rely on for a claim, a policy application, or anything else with money riding on it, get a professional valuation. Inventa's numbers are a starting point, not a substitute for one.

Exporting backups regularly, and keeping the exported file somewhere safe, is entirely on you. We are not able to recover data that was never backed up, because we never had a copy of it to begin with.
