Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Inventa keeps a home inventory on your device and nowhere else. There is no account to create, no server it talks to, and nothing about your belongings ever leaves your phone. The short version: we don't collect your data because we never receive it in the first place.

What Inventa stores, and where

Everything you put into Inventa — item names, photos, receipts, rooms, prices, warranty and service dates — is written to the app's own storage on your device: a local database and a local folder of photos. Inventa makes no network request to save, sync, or back any of it up. Nothing is uploaded, because there is nowhere in the app for it to be uploaded to.

Scanning receipts and rating plates

When you scan a receipt or a nameplate, the text is read off the photo entirely on your device using Google's ML Kit. The picture is processed locally and is never sent anywhere to be read — the recognition happens on the same phone that took the photo.

Backups

Exporting a backup writes your whole inventory — items and photos — into a single file, which the app hands to your phone's own share sheet. Where that file goes after that is entirely your choice: Files, iCloud Drive, email, a memory stick. Inventa does not upload it anywhere itself.

That file is not encrypted. Anyone who gets hold of it can open it and see the list and the photographs inside, so keep it somewhere you'd keep any other document you'd rather not lose or hand around — the protection is whatever the place you put it already gives you, not a password on the file.

The one thing that does leave your device: purchases

Inventa is sold as a one-time purchase through the App Store or Google Play. Handling that purchase is done by RevenueCat, a third-party service, on our behalf. When you buy or restore, RevenueCat receives purchase and device information and an identifier the app generates so it can tell your device is entitled to the paid features — the App Store or Play Store passes along whatever it normally does to process a payment. None of your inventory — no item, no photo, no receipt — is included in that or visible to RevenueCat. This is the only data that leaves your device, and it is limited to what is needed to unlock what you paid for.

No accounts, no analytics, no ads

There is no sign-in, no user profile, and nothing tracking how you use the app. Inventa ships with no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising software of any kind.

Children

Inventa is a general-purpose inventory tool, not directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from children — it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, since nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Deleting everything

Uninstalling Inventa deletes the database and every photo with it — permanently, since none of it exists anywhere else. If you've exported a backup and put a copy somewhere, that copy is yours to delete separately; Inventa has no way to reach it once it's left the share sheet.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Given how little the app does with data, we don't expect that to happen often.
